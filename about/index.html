My Work Expertise

Software Engineering

I design and develop interactive in-house GUIs and Computer-Aided Engineering (CAE) Software for scientific simulations, in the field of CFD and Thermal Analysis.

It involves pre- and post-processing through visual data analytics and data visualization (?). We use continuous deployment (Git) for our processes and review our development with the customer on a weekly basis.

I started running simulations as a user of CAE and analysing results with basic tools. This experience makes me now better grasp, as a developer, what is required for building intuitive and user-friendly tools in order to deliver consistent analysis in shorter timeframe.


Work style and approach

Together with the customer, we define the Software specifications in terms of interfaces, functionalities, process workflows, and external dependencies.

I then quickly come up with preliminary solutions and refine them throughout all development phases, based on feedback and testing. In particular, I like to present intermediate analysis (data frames, interactive plots) to the customer using Jupyter Notebooks. It helps visualize the sequence of processes and validate our data.

As for the code itself, I focus on maintaining a modular architecture, well-documented code (docstring, UML diagrams, Flowcharts), and user-guides.
