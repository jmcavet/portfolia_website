Fluento

Learn a new foreign language with context.

Choose the vocabulary YOU want to learn and improve your language through a fun game.

Motives for developing a brand new Language App

While learning React and Redux back in 2020, I took a chance at applying my knowledge by developing a new Progressive Web Application (PWA) for learning foreign languages.


I used Firestore and Firebase for my backend, giving me more time to focus on the frontend side. While my previous language app was not particularly solid in terms of design, I chose to implement the whole UI/UX processes in Figma. It provided me with prototypes.


How it works

Sign-up and Dashboard

Users must first signup and create an account, which information is saved in the MongoDb database. Then, they will land on the main dashboard, where they can:

Create a new project for a specific language and theme.

Manage and visualize data of a project: number of incomplete and total vocabulary, chronological entries in graphic form.

Directly complete the missing translations.

Vocabulary and Tags

For a specific project, users can save their own items:

Enter some data (word, expression, verb, etc.) and its translation (directly or complete at a later date).

Create as many tags as needed in order to regroup the items, e.g. 'Adjective', 'Verb', 'Expression', 'Slang', etc.

Manage tags: edit or delete them. It will update automatically the database.

Game

The central part of the app. It enables users to:

Filter data according to various information: tags, number of items, sampling method, their evaluation of knowledge.

Play the 'Flipping Card' game in both language directions, validate their knowledge (pass or fail), and get the final score.

Manage the item: delete it or update its information (vocabulary, translation, tags, knowledge evaluation).

Dictionary

Users can search for an item for a specific project. They can:

Look for either the vocabulary and get in return its translation, or vice-versa.
