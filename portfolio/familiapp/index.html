Familiapp

Your smart companion to plan meals, organize your recipes, and create shopping lists effortlessly.

My first implementation of the React framework

Familapp was my first project using the React framework I had just learned on Udemy. I wanted to practice building a complete frontend structure and focus on functionality over design.


Built with React and Redux, Familapp connects to Firebase Firestore for real-time data storage. Asynchronous actions and reducers manage app logic cleanly, while Material UI ensures a simple and responsive interface.


I actively use Familiapp to manage our family’s weekly meals and shopping lists. Its practical impact motivates me to expand its concept — I’m now developing Famappy, an Angular app that links recipes, ingredients, and shopping lists, while also helping organize family activities like school planning.

How it works

Sign-up and First categories

If an account already exists, users can just log in with their email.
Otherwise, they must first signup, which creates an account in the Firebase database. Once valided, they can:

Start creating new categories, such as Shopping, Home activities

For each category, create a tag (sub-category). For instance, add Vegetable, Fruit, Cheese

Start customizing your default Cart by adding your favorite ingredients for example. If you regulary plan to buy Camembert, Feta, or Goronzola cheese, add them to its corresponding tag

Recipes

Store all your favorite recipes:

First, create a new recipe by providing information such as a title, the list of ingredients, cooking steps, and the source (cooking bloc, youtube, etc.)

Optionally provide some tags to sort them out later (e.g. Fish-based, Meat-based, desert, pasta, etc.)

Once you have stored your recipes, search and filter them by name and tags

Finally, select one recipe and view its main properties provided in the first place

Meal planning

Once you have enough recipes stored, you can start planning your weekly meals:

For each lunch and dinner, either add a new meal on the fly or select one from your recipes' database, using some filter

Take advantage of an overview of all your meals for the whole week

Shopping

Get ready for shopping your ingredients:

Go to your Cart, and select your pre-defined customized ingredients you want to buy, sorted by categories. Finally, ship all those items to your shopping list

Alternatively, on the home page, add ingredients that are not already pre-defined (because they might not be often bought)

While shopping, get your list ready, sort it by categories, and delete the items you buy as you go

I use Familiapp on a weekly basis to plan family meals, organize recipes, and simplify grocery shopping. With two children and a busy schedule, it helps us stay organized and avoid last-minute meal decisions.
Beyond its practicality, it’s a project that reflects my passion for creating useful, real-life web applications that make everyday routines easier.
