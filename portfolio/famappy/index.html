Famappy

Manage your weekly family activities, such as planning meals, shopping, and scheduling school events.

Motivation for Developing a Familly Organization Application

After gaining experience with React I wanted to broaden my frontend expertise by learning Angular, a framework widely used in enterprise environments. Rather than building a tutorial project, I decided to create a complete Progressive Web Application (PWA) that addresses a real need for my own family.

The application helps organize everyday family life by allowing users to manage their own recipes, plan meals for the week, and automatically generate shopping lists based on the selected meals. It is designed to simplify meal planning while keeping all information synchronized across devices.

The goal of this project was threefold:

Create a practical application that my family and others can use every day.

Develop strong Angular skills while building a scalable application using modern technologies such as Tailwind CSS and Firebase/Firestore.

Deepen my understanding of application architecture, state management, reusable UI components, and responsive UI/UX design by developing a production-quality project from concept to deployment.

The application is still actively evolving. Planned features include school event management, allowing families to organize activities, appointments, and important dates within the same platform.

Main features of the app

Home page - dashboard

The home page acts as the central hub of the application, providing users with a quick overview of their family organization and direct access to the most important features.

Quick access to main features — Navigate easily to Meals, Shopping cart, Recipes, and other sections

Daily meal overview — View planned meals, meal types, and quick access to recipe details

Upcoming shopping lists — Quickly check and manage your next shopping cart

Recipe Suggestions — Discover ideas and get inspiration for upcoming meals

Recipes

Unlike most cooking apps, Famappy allows users to create and manage their own recipes, including ingredients, quantities, preparation steps, and additional details.

Create and manage personal recipes — Store their own recipes with ingredients, quantities, preparation steps, and other relevant information.

Access their recipe collection — Get an overview of all saved recipes in one place.

Search and filter recipes — Quickly find specific recipes by name or filter them by categories, difficulty level, cooking time, or ingredient categories.

Use recipes for meal planning — Select recipes directly when organizing weekly meals.

Meals

Famappy helps users plan their weekly meals through a calendar-based interface. Users can organize recipes for each day, assign cooks, and prepare shopping lists based on their meal plan.

Flexible meal planning — Add recipes individually or use the meal planner to select and assign multiple meals at once.

Meal assignment — Assign a cook to each meal and view this information directly on meal cards and in the weekly calendar overview.

Shopping list integration — Export selected meals from the week directly to the shopping cart, automatically preparing the required ingredients.

Shopping List

Famappy provides a flexible shopping list management system, combining automatically generated lists from meal planning with fully customizable lists created by users.

Flexible item management — Add quick items on the fly, ingredients from the recipe database with quantities, or other household items such as soap and shampoo.

Multiple list organization — Easily switch between different shopping lists and manage separate needs or occasions.

Filtering and categorization — Filter items using ingredient categories to quickly find specific products.

Shopping mode — Remove items as they are purchased to keep track of remaining items.
